About Dr. Aditya Manke

Pioneering Cancer Care with Precision

10+ Years Expert
Care

Dr. Aditya Manke is Thane's foremost Surgical Oncologist, renowned for his mastery in Robotic and Laparoscopic cancer surgeries. With a patient-first philosophy rooted in compassion and cutting-edge technique, he has transformed cancer outcomes for thousands of families across Maharashtra.

He completed his MBBS and MS (General Surgery) from the prestigious Grant Medical College & Sir J.J. Hospital, Mumbai, followed by a Surgical Oncology fellowship at Tata Medical Centre, Kolkata with a focus on Gastrointestinal Cancers.

Dr. Manke underwent advanced training in Robotic Cancer Surgery and Cytoreductive Surgery with HIPEC at The Christie NHS Hospital, Manchester, UK, and a further year at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, Delhi. He proudly holds MRCS, FRCS (Edinburgh) and FEBS (Surgical Oncology, Brussels 2016).

He leads the Sanjivani Cancer Centre in Thane and Palghar and is one of very few surgeons in India certified on both the Intuitive Da Vinci and CMR Versius robotic systems.
